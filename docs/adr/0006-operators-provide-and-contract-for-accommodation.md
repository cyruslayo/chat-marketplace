# Operators provide and contract for accommodation

For every reservation, the verified operator with documented authority over the unit is the guest's accommodation provider and contracting party. The platform is a disclosed booking marketplace and facilitator, support coordinator, and—only where confirmed by its provider contract and Nigerian counsel—limited payment-collection agent. It does not present itself as property owner, landlord, hotelier, or direct accommodation provider unless it expressly assumes that role for a particular listing.

The operator is primarily responsible for authority to offer the unit, operator-supplied facts, calendar and pricing accuracy, habitability, check-in, fulfilment, property compliance, and operator-attributable refunds. The platform remains responsible for its verification and presentation, agent-generated claims, payment orchestration, support, enforcement, and platform-attributable errors. Refund funding is allocated by cause, and nothing in this allocation limits non-excludable consumer rights or responsibility for either party's own acts, omissions, or representations.

Under Request to Book, operator confirmation creates a conditional booking offer rather than a reservation. The accommodation contract forms only when the guest accepts the final disclosed terms, backend payment verification succeeds, and the reservation is atomically committed. The booking retains immutable snapshots of the contracting operator, unit, price, policies, material rules, acceptance, confirmation, payment verification, and governing terms.
