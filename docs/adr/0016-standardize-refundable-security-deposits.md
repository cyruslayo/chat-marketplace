# Standardize refundable security deposits

Launch bookings may require one fixed, versioned NGN Refundable Security Deposit per unit within platform-defined limits. It is disclosed before Request to Book, captured in the accepted quote, collected separately from the stay payment, excluded from commission and revenue, and represented as a guest liability in the platform ledger. Operators cannot negotiate, add, collect, retain, or deduct deposits through cash, transfers, POS, messaging, ID collateral, or any process outside the platform.

For Nigeria the product assumes an actual charge and later asynchronous refund, not a card authorization hold, unless the payment provider supplies an approved local authorization product. Collection remains feature-flagged off until the selected licensed PSP and Nigerian counsel confirm a compliant structure for custody, settlement, refunds, claims, reconciliation, and liquidity. The platform must not call the arrangement escrow or route deposits through ordinary operator settlement without that approval.

After contractual checkout, an operator has 24 hours to submit a permitted, objectively assessable, evidence-backed claim; the operator cannot extend the deadline. Without a valid timely claim, the platform automatically initiates a full original-source refund. Claims exclude ordinary wear, routine cleaning, pre-existing damage, undisclosed penalties, vague inconvenience, and unsupported future revenue. The platform—not the operator or agent—adjudicates under a versioned policy, records any operator payable separately, and refunds the unapproved balance. Guest non-response never creates automatic operator entitlement.
