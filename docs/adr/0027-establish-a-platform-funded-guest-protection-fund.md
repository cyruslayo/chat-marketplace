# Establish a platform-funded Guest Protection Fund

The platform establishes a Guest Protection Fund from initial company capital and a later-defined share of earned commission. It provides prompt refund liquidity, emergency relocation, essential relocation transport, and reasonable replacement-stay price differences for eligible, verified or strongly substantiated operator failures while operator or PSP recovery is delayed. Contributions begin only when commission becomes earned; guest payments, deposits, Operator Net, Rolling Reserves, taxes, and funds pending fulfilment never fund it.

The fund is platform capital under versioned eligibility, approval, recovery, and reporting rules. It is not customer money, escrow, an operator reserve, working capital, insurance, or an unconditional guarantee, and no agent may disburse it. Authorized humans approve deterministic workflows under tiered limits. Platform-caused remedies use platform capital but are tracked separately so the fund does not conceal product defects.

Fund use creates and pursues operator liability through unsettled earnings, Rolling Reserves, future payouts, negative balances, and contractual recovery; recovered money replenishes the fund. Every use triggers operator-risk reassessment and reports guest resolution speed separately from underlying failure. Repeated or severe failures lead to restrictions or suspension rather than continued subsidy. Funding rates, seed capital, minimum balance, booking caps, relocation limits, accounting structure, public name, and insurance-law implications require unit-economics, PSP, finance, and Nigerian legal review before promotion.
