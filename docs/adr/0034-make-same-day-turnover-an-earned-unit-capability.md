# Make same-day turnover an earned unit capability

Same-Day Turnover is disabled by default per unit: the checkout date cannot accept a new arrival, so the earliest next check-in is the following date. The platform may grant a unit approval only after demonstrated cleaning, inspection, access preparation, inventory control, contact responsiveness, and contingency readiness. Approval belongs to the unit rather than operator and records its earliest permitted same-day arrival time.

An approved unit follows a versioned Turnover Plan with primary and backup cleaning responsibility, expected task durations, structured readiness evidence, essential utility and access checks, and human escalation before the incoming window. An incoming same-day reservation fixes the prior checkout at 11:00 AM and prohibits Late Checkout. The platform never offers an arrival earlier than the approved plan allows.

Serious or repeated late readiness, failed access, cleanliness, maintenance, checklist, responsiveness, displacement, or relocation failures suspend eligibility. Operator or material cleaning changes, renovation, inspection expiry, and extended inactivity expire or reopen approval. Guests receive accurate arrival expectations; the platform need not market the internal capability as a trust badge.
