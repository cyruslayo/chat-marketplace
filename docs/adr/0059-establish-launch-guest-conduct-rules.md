# Establish launch guest conduct rules

Launch prohibits parties, events, undisclosed commercial use, indoor smoking or vaping, excessive noise, dangerous or unlawful conduct, and occupancy beyond the verified maximum. Every overnight occupant is named; visitors are either prohibited per unit or registered between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM within total capacity; pets are prohibited by default and allowed only under disclosed Pet Friendly rules; children are allowed absent a documented inspection-supported restriction; quiet hours are 10:00 PM–8:00 AM.

The primary guest presents a platform credential and approved photo ID for visual comparison, while operators cannot copy or retain identity evidence without legal requirement. Breaches receive proportionate warning, cure, human review, protective action, or termination by severity. Money consequences are limited to contracted charges and reasonable evidenced loss through platform process; operators create no cash fines.
