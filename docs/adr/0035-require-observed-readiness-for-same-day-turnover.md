# Require observed readiness for Same-Day Turnover

A unit becomes eligible for Same-Day Turnover review only after five completed platform stays and three successful platform-observed Turnover Runs within the preceding 90 days. At least two runs follow real guest stays; the third may be a supervised simulation or full workflow inspection while the next arrival date remains blocked. A qualifying stay reaches Verified Access and Contractual Checkout without operator cancellation or unresolved material incident and completes its post-checkout reporting period.

Each successful run executes the actual Turnover Plan from checkout through cleaning, inspection, advertised utility and amenity checks, damage recording, and prepared tested access, with reachable primary or backup staff. It finishes at least 30 minutes before the unit's proposed same-day Check-In Window. Qualification also requires no attributable failed access, serious cleanliness, substitution, habitability, turnover delay, or material listing incident; at least 90% timely acknowledgement of operational messages; no missed urgent escalation; and reachable staff during every observed run.

The numeric threshold creates eligibility for human operational review, not automatic approval. Review verifies confirmed primary and backup staffing, may require remediation, and assigns a unit-specific earliest same-day arrival time. Serious incidents pause or reset the clean qualification period; minor defects fail the observation and require documented correction. Thresholds remain versioned and provisional until operating data validates their predictive value.
