# Review predictable booking risk before disclosure

When available facts predict Human Risk Review, a Request Draft enters review before operator disclosure, inventory blocking, or payment. The operator sees nothing and the guest is clearly told price and availability remain unreserved. AI may collect permitted facts, identify triggers, and summarize evidence but cannot decide. Rejection closes the draft without operator impact.

Approval is scoped and time-limited to the reviewed guest, payer context, unit, dates, party, value tolerance, evidence, and policy version; material changes invalidate it. Approval itself reserves nothing. Before disclosure the platform freshly validates publication, inventory, quote, policies, identity, operator eligibility, and booking rules and obtains renewed guest acceptance for material changes.

New risk appearing after disclosure never extends existing operator or payment deadlines. Post-initiation review must approve before the applicable inventory cutoff; rejection or unresolved review releases dates and fully refunds any successful payment. Risk checks that predict human work should therefore run before disclosure. Decisions use documented relevant factors, human authority, evidence provenance, reason codes, audit history, and minimum operator disclosure.
