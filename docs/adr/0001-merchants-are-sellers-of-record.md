# Merchants are the sellers of record

Each merchant is the seller of record for goods supplied through its stores. The platform operates discovery, checkout orchestration, payment collection, support tooling, and merchant-order coordination in return for commission, but does not sell the goods. This aligns contractual sale, fulfilment, returns, warranties, product compliance, and refund funding with the merchant that controls the offer; marketplace surfaces must therefore identify that merchant clearly.

Payment implementation must not silently contradict this commercial model. Whether a merchant is also the payment-network merchant of record remains subject to the payment-provider contract and must not be inferred from a split-payment or subaccount API.
