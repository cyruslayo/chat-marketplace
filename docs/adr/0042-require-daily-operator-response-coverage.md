# Require daily operator response coverage

Every operator with published requestable inventory provides authorized booking-response coverage from 8:00 AM through 8:00 PM WAT every day using staff rotation, a tested backup, and escalation contact. Coverage means someone with authority can confirm or decline requests, maintain the calendar, attest price and unit accuracy, report operational problems, and escalate incidents—not merely that messages can be delivered. Operators may extend but never shorten the minimum.

Only a Booking Request whose full 30-minute response window fits inside Active Hours may be disclosed, making 7:30 PM the latest normal disclosure. Later work remains a non-blocking Request Draft for revalidation at 8:00 AM. Confirmation near closing may create a Payment Pending window beyond 8:00 PM because the operator decision is complete.

Active Hours are distinct from arrival fulfilment and platform incident coverage. An operator whose unit permits later arrival maintains designated check-in contacts through the complete Contractual Check-In Window. When minimum staffing is unavailable, the operator or platform pauses new requestable inventory while preserving every existing booking and incident obligation. Metrics and enforcement distinguish operator timeout, invalid contact details, approved closure, and platform delivery failure.
