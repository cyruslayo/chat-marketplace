# Standardize checkout at 11:00 AM WAT

Every launch reservation has an 11:00 AM West Africa Time Contractual Checkout. Operators cannot configure an earlier or later standard time; operators needing more turnover time narrow their unit's Check-In Window instead. The platform generates the exact timestamp from the checkout date and Africa/Lagos timezone and displays it before Request to Book and payment.

Late checkout requires operator approval, calendar and incoming-booking validation, staffed support, deterministic pricing when charged, explicit guest acceptance, and an immutable Checkout Amendment. The amended time starts applicable post-checkout deadlines, including the 24-hour deposit-claim window. Informal messages do not amend the contract.

Early physical departure does not automatically alter Contractual Checkout or claim timing. Guest confirmation helps turnover but silence does not delay the deadline. Unauthorized overstay opens an incident; operators cannot impose cash or arbitrary penalties, and any recovery follows a standardized disclosed policy and evidence-backed claim process.
