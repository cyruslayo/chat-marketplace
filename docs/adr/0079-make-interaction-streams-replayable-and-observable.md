# Make interaction streams replayable and observable

Interaction delivery is ordered, replayable and at least once. Events carry stable identity, sequence, time and trace correlation; clients deduplicate, reject conflicting duplicates and resynchronize on gaps. SSE heartbeats, bounded reconnect, durable staging, compaction, payload and rate limits, timeouts and backpressure controls prevent transport failure from corrupting workflow state. Timeouts never reverse committed commands.

The provisional launch profile uses 15-second heartbeats; 64 KiB ordinary events; 256 KiB complete surfaces; 10 MiB un-compacted run streams; 20 events/second sustained with a 50 events/second five-second burst; 10-second establishment, 120-second standard-run, 300-second incident-run, 30-second idle-warning and 60-second idle-termination limits. Initial monthly objectives are 99.9% gateway availability and valid-surface rendering, p95 under two seconds for stream establishment and command acknowledgement, under four seconds to first visible response, under three seconds for reconnect restoration, and zero lost durable events or duplicate command side effects.

Launch maintains end-to-end correlation, redacted metrics and material-action audit evidence plus golden streams, schema, malicious-input, reconnect, duplicate, stale-surface, channel-parity, accessibility and CopilotKit-independent reference-client fixtures. The numeric limits, timeouts and initial SLOs remain provisional engineering targets. Protocol, renderer, framework or model-runtime upgrades require replay, semantic-command parity, safe rejection, accessibility and security checks, and verified rollback.
