# Let guests choose relocation or refund

When an operator cannot provide the contracted unit before or at check-in, the guest chooses either a platform-approved Comparable Replacement at no additional cost, with reasonable failure-caused transport, or a full original-source refund. This applies to operator cancellation, unavailable or double-booked units, failed access, unreachability, substitution, material inaccuracy, uninhabitable or seriously unsafe conditions, and loss of authority. It does not apply to failure caused solely by the guest.

Comparability is assessed against the original contract and the guest's disclosed essentials, including dates, Entire Place status, capacity, bedrooms and beds, required amenities and accessibility, verification status, practical location, quality, and acceptable check-in timing. The platform may recommend and arrange options but cannot force another city or materially different area, inferior capacity or quality, shared accommodation, missing essentials, additional cost, changed dates, significant delay, or a replacement from an operator the guest no longer trusts. Consent is specific, authenticated, and records the replacement terms; silence and general substitution clauses are not consent.

If the platform cannot promptly provide a genuinely comparable option, refund is the default. A full refund covers all collected booking amounts, unsupplied extras, and the security deposit; ordinary commission is unearned. Relocation uses the Guest Protection Fund for eligible bridging costs and creates operator recovery without releasing liability. The guest receives one primary accommodation remedy, while reasonable transport already caused by the failure may remain reimbursable. Operators cannot substitute units directly; every proposal passes platform review and explicit guest acceptance.
