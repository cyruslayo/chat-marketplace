# Require the primary guest to be the payer

The verified primary guest must also be the payer for launch bookings. Payments from relatives, friends, employers, companies, assistants, operators, or other third parties are unsupported. Before payment the guest makes a structured Payer Attestation, but attestation and payment success are not proof of payer identity; a reservation is not confirmed until required channel-specific payer attribution has completed.

Attribution uses verified bank-account identity where available, processor authentication, payment-method evidence, risk controls, and manual review. It must not rely on universal exact-name matching because Nigerian payment channels expose different identity evidence and legitimate names vary in ordering, middle names, initials, surnames, and provider formatting. A material mismatch or insufficient evidence under the applicable risk policy pauses confirmation; review may approve a genuine identity variance, request another method or evidence, or refund and reject a third-party payment. Operators never receive payer identity evidence or internal risk findings.

Cards, bank-based payments, and USSD may remain available according to their actual attribution capabilities. Cash, direct operator transfers, informal POS collection, screenshots, crypto, and conversational claims of payment are unsupported. Refunds return to the original payment source wherever possible; any exceptional bank payout requires controlled identity and finance review and never follows an unverified replacement-account instruction.
