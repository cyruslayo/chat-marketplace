# Require versioned booking amendments

Dates, price, occupants, checkout, and other material terms change only through an atomic, versioned platform amendment; external messages change nothing and the original booking remains valid until completion. Date changes must begin at least 24 hours before check-in; extensions begin by 6:00 PM the day before checkout and complete by 8:00 PM; every change revalidates availability, price, 14-night aggregation, 90-day horizon, inspection, authority and payment, with refunds or additional payment applied to the accepted amendment.

Primary-guest replacement remains prohibited. Late first access after 10:00 PM is only a supported human-approved self-check-in exception, not a product promise. Unauthorized overstay begins at 11:00 AM, uses disclosed late-checkout pricing or evidenced cost before 2:00 PM, and after 2:00 PM may create one additional nightly amount plus evidenced direct losses without double recovery or arbitrary penalty.
