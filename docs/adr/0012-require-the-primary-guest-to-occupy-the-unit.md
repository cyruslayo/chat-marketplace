# Require the primary guest to occupy the unit

Every launch reservation is a Self-Booking: the verified primary guest must be named on the reservation, attest that they will occupy the unit, be present at check-in, and remain responsible for all declared occupants. Third-party, gift, assistant, corporate, travel-agent, and family bookings where the verified account holder will not stay are prohibited. This maintains a direct relationship between the verified identity, booking contract, and person receiving accommodation.

The attestation is captured as structured, versioned acceptance rather than inferred from conversation. Operators see that the primary guest must attend check-in but never receive platform-held identity evidence. An absent primary guest pauses check-in and requires platform review; the operator cannot substitute another guest informally or through an external message.

Changing the primary guest requires a replacement account, contact and government-ID verification, a new occupancy attestation, fresh risk checks, and operator approval where the request has already been accepted. The original identity assurance and contractual position are not transferable. Non-self bookings may later be introduced as explicit booking-party types with separate booker, payer, guest, authorization, consent, refund, and liability rules.
