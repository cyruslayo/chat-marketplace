# Cap the booking horizon at ninety days

Launch check-in dates are limited to a rolling 90-calendar-day Booking Horizon calculated in Africa/Lagos time. Units may expose a shorter but never longer horizon without ranking penalty. The limit applies to check-in, so a valid fourteen-night stay may check out after Day 90. Dates beyond the effective horizon cannot be searched as requestable, held, disclosed, confirmed, paid, or manually overridden.

The entire stay must remain within every applicable inspection, management-authority, regulatory, operator-approval, support, maintenance, pricing, and operational validity boundary. The effective limit is the earliest of those boundaries, the platform horizon, and unit configuration. Every exposed date has deterministic all-in pricing; missing pricing means unavailable.

Eligibility is rechecked at search, draft, disclosure, confirmation, and payment initialization. A later configuration reduction affects only new requests and never cancels an existing contract, while later safety, authority, or compliance facts retain their independent protective effect. Changes to price or policy do not rewrite captured booking terms.
