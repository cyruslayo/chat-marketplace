# Permit same-day bookings without shortcuts

Same-day arrivals are permitted when the Booking Request reaches Pending Operator by the unit's Latest Disclosure Cutoff and every ordinary identity, risk, availability, quote, Active Hours, operator confirmation, payment, access preparation, Human Incident Support, and readiness control passes unchanged. Urgency never permits Instant Book, direct operator payment, reduced review, cutoff override, informal confirmation, substitution, or compressed deadlines.

A unit without a checkout that morning may proceed only when currently clean, habitable, maintained, accessible, supported, and uncommitted. A unit checking out that morning requires active unit-level Same-Day Turnover approval and an arrival at or after its approved earliest time; its established Readiness Deadline and escalation workflow remain authoritative.

Search presents same-day bookability only when the complete safe transaction and operational path remains possible. After cutoff, no operator claim that the unit is ready creates an exception; the guest receives later-date or later-window alternatives without being directed off-platform.
