# Require Management Authority through checkout

At Booking Request disclosure and contract formation, verified property-specific Management Authority must expressly cover shortlet advertising, booking acceptance, guest contracting and access, operator revenue, cancellations, refunds, and incidents through Contractual Checkout. Pending, expected, negotiating, submitted, verbal, unsigned, or otherwise unapproved renewal creates no provisional eligibility and cannot manually open dates.

Authority is revalidated throughout search, drafting, disclosure, confirmation, payment initialization, and contract formation. Known expiry before checkout makes the stay unavailable; loss before payment stops checkout, releases inventory, takes no money, and closes as Authority Ineligible rather than operator decline. The booking snapshots the approved authority version and grantor relationship.

Unexpected revocation, dispute, or material restriction after confirmation pauses new sales and relevant payouts and opens booking-level review of lawful fulfilment, access, safety, and existing-reservation scope. A replacement operator receives no silent transfer: it completes verification and fresh authority, and each existing booking receives lawful assignment review, material disclosure, and guest acceptance where continuation is possible; otherwise relocation or refund applies.
