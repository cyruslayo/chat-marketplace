# Limit launch stays to fourteen nights

Launch supports Short Stays of one through fourteen consecutive nights. The authoritative booking service rejects longer ranges before operator search, quoting, identity verification, inventory blocking, or payment. The platform does not negotiate, refer, quote, or facilitate longer occupancy through messaging. Operators may configure a permitted unit minimum but cannot raise the platform maximum.

Continuous Occupancy at the same property is aggregated across adjacent reservations and unit changes when it involves the same primary guest or materially the same party. Extensions require availability, a new quote, Request to Book approval, updated terms, and verified payment, while the combined occupancy remains within fourteen nights. The platform and operator cannot encourage fragmentation or informal extensions to evade the boundary.

Fourteen nights is an operational product limit, not a statutory tenancy safe harbor. Extended stays remain a separately designed future capability requiring Nigerian legal review and dedicated contracts, pricing, payment and payout schedules, servicing, utilities, cancellation, deposits, occupant changes, and support rules.
