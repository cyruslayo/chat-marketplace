# Expire risk reviews at the earliest safe deadline

A pre-request Human Risk Review expires at the earlier of 24 hours after opening or the booking's latest safe disclosure cutoff. The cutoff reserves time for renewed guest consent, availability and quote validation, Technical Delivery Window, operator response, Payment Window, possible grace, and confirmation before check-in. Requests for more information, reviewer delay, and senior intervention do not reset or extend the effective deadline.

Unresolved expiry closes the Request Draft, stops review work and links, notifies the guest that nothing was sent, held, or charged, and creates no operator or calendar event. It is recorded as Expired Unresolved rather than Risk Rejected so future evaluation and fairness reporting remain accurate. A review that cannot fit safely is never bypassed because arrival is urgent.

A later attempt receives fresh account and policy evaluation, inventory and price validation, and a newly scoped review where required, while lawfully retained still-valid identity assurance may be reused. The prior unresolved outcome does not automatically reject the guest.
