# Require passed inspection through checkout

At Booking Request disclosure and contract formation, a unit must have an approved passed physical inspection whose validity extends through Contractual Checkout. Scheduled, requested, in-progress, evidence-pending, conditional, or unapproved reinspection creates no provisional eligibility and cannot manually open future dates. A passed reinspection extends inventory only after approval, recording, complete revalidation, and normal guest consent and disclosure flow.

Inspection eligibility is checked throughout search, drafting, disclosure, confirmation, payment initialization, and contract formation. Loss before payment stops checkout, releases inventory, takes no money, and closes as Inspection Ineligible rather than operator decline or timeout. Each contract snapshots the inspection record and validity used.

Known routine expiry cannot affect a formed booking because stays crossing it never qualify. Unexpected early invalidation after confirmation—such as material change, serious damage, safety evidence, utility loss, or unreliable inspection—follows ADR-0009's booking-specific risk review and may result in preservation, urgent reinspection, relocation, or refund rather than automatic cancellation.
