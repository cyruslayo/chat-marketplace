# Require rolling reserves for all operators

Every active launch operator has a non-zero Rolling Reserve from its first paid booking, including established businesses and operators with strong off-platform reputations. Revenue Release makes Operator Net payable but actual settlement applies negative-balance recovery, a booking-level Reserve Tranche, and other authorized account offsets first. Reserves cover only defined operator-attributable refunds, chargebacks, relocation and fulfilment failures, misrepresentation, approved claim reversals, processor costs, and negative balances—not platform expenses, failures, penalties, or guest deposit claims awaiting adjudication.

A versioned commercial-risk policy sets percentage, minimum balance, holding period, release conditions, and risk tier. New or higher-risk operators may have larger or longer requirements and restricted accounts may have payouts paused. Each tranche remains traceable to its booking; reaching its nominal date creates eligibility for review, not unconditional payment. Release also requires an active account, no negative balance or relevant open incident, and satisfaction of the continuing minimum. Active operators do not reach zero reserve merely because individual tranches mature.

The operator agreement authorizes transparent contributions, defined applications, negative-balance recovery, risk-based changes, payout holds, and post-termination retention. Actual funds remain within a PSP-approved, counsel-reviewed structure; Rolling Reserve is an internal and contractual restriction on operator earnings, not escrow, guest funds, insurance, or platform revenue. Activation remains blocked until the PSP confirms that operator-level delayed payout and reserve treatment is permitted.
