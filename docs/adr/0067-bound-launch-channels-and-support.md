# Bound launch channels and support

The backend booking domain is authoritative. Web supports the complete guest transaction and sensitive workflows; WhatsApp supports bounded discovery, drafts, status, reminders, structured operator request/calendar/readiness actions, incident triage and handoff, redirecting identity, payment credentials, account changes, high-risk evidence and material amendments to authenticated surfaces. Instagram is acquisition and deep-linking only and cannot request, hold, pay, confirm or carry sensitive evidence.

General human support runs 8:00 AM–8:00 PM daily with a 15-minute response target; check-in coverage runs approximately 1:00 PM–midnight or until every active incident is safely owned; active stays have 24/7 on-call escalation with ten-minute overnight and five-minute check-in-window ownership targets. Material safety, access, relocation, refund, identity, payer, payment inconsistency, deposit, substitution, cancellation, authority, discrimination and fraud cases require human handoff. Channels share incident state, use at least two tested operator routes, preserve separate delivery/read/response events, and never turn free text into authoritative state without structured confirmation and backend validation.
