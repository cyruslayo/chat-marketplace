# Cap late checkout at 2:00 PM WAT

Late Checkout may amend departure only to 12:00 PM, 1:00 PM, or 2:00 PM West Africa Time; 2:00 PM is the absolute launch limit. Any confirmed reservation beginning on the checkout date makes Late Checkout unavailable regardless of its expected arrival time. Approval additionally requires no conflicting maintenance or inspection, confirmed operator turnover capacity, staffed platform support, a revised quote for any fee, and explicit guest acceptance.

The booking service—not an operator or agent—enforces eligibility and records the capacity checks, operator approval, policy version, price, and guest acceptance. An operator may offer a shorter extension than requested but cannot create arbitrary times or amend through external messaging. The amended time governs reminders, access expiry, turnover, overstay, deposit-claim timing, and incident coverage.

After 2:00 PM, continued occupancy is an unauthorized overstay rather than an extendable late checkout. The platform opens an incident and applies only standardized, evidence-backed remedies; operators cannot collect cash, direct transfers, or arbitrary penalties.
