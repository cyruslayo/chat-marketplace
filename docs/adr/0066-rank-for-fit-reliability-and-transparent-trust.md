# Rank for fit, reliability and transparent trust

Organic discovery prioritizes eligibility, guest fit, all-in value, current verification, calendar and fulfilment reliability, response performance, verified-stay reviews, readiness, listing freshness and expressed preferences. Initial launch has no sponsored placement. Trust surfaces show specific claims and, after ten opportunities, trailing-90-day reliability metrics rather than a generic Verified badge; lifetime completed stays and current inspection/authority status remain separately clear.

Only a primary guest from a paid completed Verified Access stay may leave one review within 14 days; publication follows operator response or window expiry, operators receive one response, incentives and imported reviews are prohibited, and moderation addresses privacy, threats, extortion, irrelevance and fabrication rather than negative opinion. Before booking the product shows neighbourhood and roughly 750m-obscured map location; full address follows confirmed payment; access instructions release no earlier than 24 hours before check-in after blockers clear, or after readiness for same-day bookings. Material disclosures never live only in chat.
