# Support non-exclusive inventory with authoritative blocks

Launch inventory is non-exclusive. Operators may accept bookings through other channels without ranking penalty or disclosure of external guest or commercial details, but must create an authoritative Operator Block for the unit and dates before confirming the external commitment. A low-friction Operator Hold may temporarily protect dates during an external enquiry and then converts to a block or expires. Dashboard, structured WhatsApp actions, calendar sync, and authorized staff all update the same central Availability Calendar rather than leaving state in conversation.

Blocks cannot silently displace platform commitments. With no active request they take effect; an unconfirmed request requires explicit decline and records a calendar-accuracy event; an operator-confirmed or payment-pending conflict requires human incident handling; and a paid booking cannot be overridden. Request pending, payment pending, booked, operator blocked, and maintenance states all prevent overlap under one effective availability result.

Accurately blocked external business carries no penalty. Omitted or stale availability is classified by impact: correction before a request, unavailable-request decline, post-confirmation conflict, or paid-booking cancellation/displacement. Enforcement considers severity, recurrence, absolute count, and rate without double-counting one underlying incident. External integrations assist but do not remove operator responsibility, and unhealthy sync is disclosed and handled conservatively.
