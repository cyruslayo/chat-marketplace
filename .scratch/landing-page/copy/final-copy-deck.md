## Answer change needed (fact reconciliation, panels 3 and 5)

Q1's current answer ("No. The dates are yours once…") reads as contradicting beat 4's 30-minute hold. Proposed:
"Not yet. They're held for you while the Operator replies, up to 30 minutes in their active hours, and they're yours once the Operator confirms and your payment is verified."
(ADR-0041: a disclosed request blocks the dates for 30 minutes during active hours; ADR-0005: only a verified payment makes a Reservation.)

## Recommended additions (beyond wording; owner decides)

1. **"Nothing due yet" status tag per beat** (panels 1 and 2, merged). Each beat caption carries a small status: beats 1–4 say "Nothing due yet"; beat 5 says "Due now: the amount on your offer". It answers the visitor's constant "am I committed?" question. It's one small kit status tag per caption, and the app screens are untouched.
2. **A forwardable three-line promise under the hero** (panel 4): "Say it once. See the full price. Pay after the yes." Each line is a true step. Designed to be screenshotted and shared on WhatsApp.
3. **"What we won't do" block before the FAQ** (panel 3): "We won't send anything you haven't checked. We won't take payment before the Operator says yes. We won't show apartments you can't book. We won't hide the deposit inside the total." It ends with honest limits: "Lagos and Abuja for now, stays up to 14 nights."
