# Panel 1: Anxiety, not speed

Lens: the booker of a short-let in Lagos or Abuja is less worried about how fast they can book than about the unknown steps: whether the price will grow, whether the place is still free, whether the money went through, and where the request sits right now. Each line below tries to make one of those uncertainties visibly smaller.

## 1. Insight

- The biggest fear is paying for a place that is gone or a total that grows at the end. Copy should show the number and the status before anyone is asked to commit.
- Waiting is the real stress. A visitor who has sent a request and hears nothing feels lost. "Where you stand" matters more than "how soon".
- "Did my money go through?" is an open loop that stays open for hours. Name the moment the payment is verified and the moment the Reservation is confirmed.
- Agents on WhatsApp teach people to expect vague replies and silence. Concrete, dated certainty ("Reply due by 10:45") is a relief, not a detail.
- Calm comes from knowing what will NOT happen (nothing sent before you press send, nothing due if the Operator declines). Negative certainty is a strong form of reassurance.

## 4. Bold idea

Put a "where your booking stands" line under each beat in the phone and in the caption: one fixed status sentence that says what is happening now and what is not yet due (for example, "Nothing is due yet. The Operator has until 10:45 am."). This moves the reassurance from copy about the product to a live answer to the visitor's constant question, and it reuses facts already in the app (reply deadline, nothing due if declined, nothing sent until you press send), so it stays inside the approved claims.
