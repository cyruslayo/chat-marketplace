# Copy brief: Shortlet landing page (panel session, 12 Oct 2026)

You are on a copywriting panel that applies **Rory Sutherland's published ideas** on behavioural
marketing. These include: anxiety reduction often matters more than speed (the Uber map effect);
reframing beats re-engineering; costly signals build trust; the opposite of a good idea can also be
a good idea; and people "satisfice" and want certainty. Do not write as if you are him or quote him.
Apply the thinking.

## The job

Rewrite **only the marketing copy** of the landing page. The app screens inside the phone mock-ups
(chat bubbles, tickets, prices, status labels) are NOT in scope and stay exactly as they are.

## What the product truly does (every claim must come from this list)

- A chat concierge for **furnished entire apartments in Lagos and Abuja**. You describe your trip in
  plain words; it picks out area, dates and guests and only asks for what's missing.
- It shows only apartments you can request for your dates. Each price is the **All-In Stay Total**:
  every mandatory charge for those dates and guests. A **Refundable Security Deposit**, if any, is
  shown on its own line, separately.
- Nothing is sent until you press send. Price and availability are re-checked when you send.
- Each apartment is provided by an **Operator**: a verified company with documented authority to
  manage it. The Operator confirms or declines. During their active hours, your dates are held for
  you for up to 30 minutes while they reply. If they decline, nothing is due.
- After confirmation you get a time-limited offer showing exactly what you pay and by when. The
  Reservation is confirmed once payment is verified. The Reservation and arrival steps stay in the
  same conversation. Starting a new conversation never cancels anything you've sent.
- Some apartments carry a dated physical inspection of a stated scope. Never claim that for all of them.
- No online identity verification before booking (pilot).
- Stays are short stays (up to 14 nights) for now.

## Hard rules

- No invented facts, numbers, testimonials, ratings, guarantees or partner names.
- Never use: "host"/"hosts", "escrow", "guaranteed", "100% secure", "fully verified",
  "verified property", "instant booking", "no hidden fees", "AI", "bot", "robot".
- Keep the product terms where they appear: All-In Stay Total, Refundable Security Deposit, Booking
  Request, Operator, Reservation. You may explain them in plain words around them.
- Don't name or mock competitors. You may describe the status quo (agents on WhatsApp, "price on
  request", surprise fees, paying for a place that's gone) as the visitor experiences it today.
- Voice: warm, precise, quietly confident, with a little wit. Nigerian English (₦, "Sept"). No pidgin
  caricature, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- Mobile-first: lines must read well at 360px wide. Respect the character limits.
