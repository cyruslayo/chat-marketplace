# Define the "house-hunt while you stay" service (segment 2)

Status: ready-for-human
Type: grilling
Blocked by: 01

## Why

Relocating guests stay with us while they look for a long lease, and house-hunting in Lagos and Abuja is full of scams. A paid, verified viewing service reuses the inspection capability, gives the platform a second source of revenue, and keeps the guest with us until they move.

## Decisions needed (after the pilot shows demand)

1. What the service is: shortlisted listings, accompanied viewings, title and ownership checks (C of O), and/or negotiation support.
2. Who performs it: our inspectors, or partner agents. What liability we take, and what we do not.
3. How it is priced: a fixed fee, a per-viewing fee, or a success fee. Whether it is sold only to Extended Stay guests.
4. Whether it needs its own ADR (it is a different service from accommodation) and its own glossary terms.
5. Whether pilot guests asked for it, and how many would pay (from issue 01 AC3).

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: A decision recorded under `## Answer`: build, pilot by hand, or drop.
- AC2: If build: a scoped follow-up issue (or a separate PRD) with ADR needs listed.
