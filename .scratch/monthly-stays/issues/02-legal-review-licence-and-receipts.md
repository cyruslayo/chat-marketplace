# Nigerian legal and tax review: licence to occupy vs tenancy, and stay receipts

Status: ready-for-human
Type: research
Blocked by: none
Requested: 3 Oct 2026

## Why

ADR 0023 says a fourteen-night cap is "not a statutory tenancy safe harbor". It also says extended stays need Nigerian legal review before they launch. If an Extended Stay counts as a tenancy, the guest may gain statutory protection: a notice to quit and a court process for recovering possession. That changes what happens when a period payment is missed or a guest overstays. Segment 1 also needs receipts an employer will accept, and the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 took effect in January 2026.

## Questions for counsel (Lagos and FCT)

1. Under the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 (and the Tenancy Bill 2025 if enacted) and the FCT Recovery of Premises Act, what makes a furnished, serviced stay of 30–90 nights (ADR 0092) a **licence to occupy** rather than a tenancy? What should the Booking Contract say, and what must operations actually do to stay on the licence side? Examples: services provided, no exclusive possession beyond the guest party, access for cleaning.
2. If a later Billing Period goes unpaid, what may lawfully happen at the end of the paid period, and what may not? Specifically: no lockout, no removal of belongings, and what notice is required.
3. Is there a maximum length beyond which a licence is unsafe, even with services?
4. Under ADR 0006 the owner or operator is the Accommodation Provider and the platform collects as agent (ADR 0089). Does collecting one period at a time change the agency analysis or the Paystack merchant agreement?
5. **Receipts:**
   - What must a stay receipt show to be accepted as an employee expense in Nigeria?
   - Must VAT or consumption tax be shown or charged on the platform margin or on the accommodation, and by whom?
   - Does the NTA 2025 change this?
6. Are Lagos or FCT registrations or levies triggered by stays of 30 nights or more (ADR 0065)? Does the Banana Island-style estate ban pattern affect particular estates?
7. Does the cancellation and notice approach in issue 03 need anything for consumer law (ADR 0014 requires legal approval of thresholds)?
8. **Non-circumvention (issue 15).** Can an operator agreement lawfully bar the operator from contracting directly with a platform-introduced guest for about 12 months, and can a fee be charged if they do? What wording and period will hold up, and can any similar promise be asked of the guest?
9. **Exclusivity and rate parity (issue 15).** If an operator agrees to sell Extended Stays only through the platform, or not to undercut the platform's price, does that raise concerns under the FCCPA 2018? Does the answer change with the platform's share of a neighbourhood's supply?
10. **Central pricing (issue 15, strategy page section 6).** The platform holds many competing operators' prices. If it sets guest prices within operator-set floors and ceilings and shares revenue above a baseline, could the FCCPC treat that as algorithmic price coordination? What safeguards are needed, for example independent operator floors, no sharing of one operator's prices with another, and pricing as agent?
11. **Revenue floor (issue 15, Guaranteed tier).** If the platform guarantees an operator a minimum quarterly revenue and tops up any shortfall, does that change the agency analysis in question 4, make the platform the Accommodation Provider or a lessee, or create licensing or tax consequences?
