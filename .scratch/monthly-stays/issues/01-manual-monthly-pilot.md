# Run the 60-day manual monthly pilot

Status: ready-for-human
Type: task
Blocked by: none
Requested: 3 Oct 2026

## Why

Show that segments 1 (assignment professionals) and 2 (relocating people) will pay for monthly stays before the product is built. The evidence also tells issue 03 which values to choose.

## What to do

1. **Supply.** Sign 10–15 units: about half in Abuja (Wuse 2, Maitama, Jabi, Gwarinpa) and half in Lagos (Lekki Phase 1, VI, Ikeja GRA, Yaba).
   - Each unit gets a passed Physical Inspection using today's workflow.
   - Record each owner's monthly agreed amount and what it includes: power and generator fuel, water, internet and cleaning.
   - Owner pitch: per-night income at today's occupancy versus a guaranteed month. For example, ₦80k a night at 43% occupancy is about ₦1.03M a month.
2. **Demand.** Put up one landing page with two entry points, "On assignment" and "Relocating", the same monthly price card, and a waitlist. Tag every lead by segment.
   - Channels: LinkedIn, Abuja NGO and expat WhatsApp groups, consulting networks, HR contacts.
3. **Stays.**
   - Book the first 14 nights through today's Short Stay flow, which is the only flow allowed (ADR 0023).
   - Document anything longer under a written agreement reviewed by counsel (issue 02), outside the platform.
   - Do **not** split one stay into back-to-back 14-night bookings. ADR 0023 forbids fragmentation to get around the cap.
   - If counsel is not ready, limit pilot stays on the platform to 14 nights and treat the monthly offer as a waitlist only.
4. **Measure** for 60 days and record the results under `## Answer`.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: Results recorded for each signal:
  - paid monthly stays (target ≥10);
  - extend or rebook rate (target ≥40%);
  - referrals per guest (target ≥1 in 3);
  - inbound company or NGO admin requests (target ≥2);
  - owner renewal (target ≥70%).
- AC2: Leads and stays split by segment (assignment / relocating / diaspora).
- AC3: For each stay: length, how the guest paid, whether they needed a receipt for an employer, and what went wrong mid-stay (power, water, cleaning, maintenance).
- AC4: A recommendation for each issue 03 question (stay length, period length, due date, notice, cancellation, deposit), backed by this evidence.

## ADR compliance

| ADR | Constraint |
|---|---|
| 0023 | No stay over 14 nights on the platform, and no fragmentation |
| 0008, 0056 | Units inspected before listing; inspection valid through checkout |
| 0012, 0013 | Self-Booking; the guest is the payer |
| 0089 | Owner's agreed amounts plus margin; manual owner payouts |
