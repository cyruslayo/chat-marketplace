# Write the identity-at-check-in procedure

Status: ready-for-human
Type: grilling
Blocked by: none

## What to decide

ADR 0085 moves guest identity checks to check-in but deliberately leaves the procedure unwritten. Access must not be granted until the procedure exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Who checks ID, when (before arrival or at the door), and which documents are accepted.
- [ ] What happens if the guest fails or refuses the check (refund outcome per the cancellation catalogue).
- [ ] How the result is recorded without storing more identity data than needed (ADR 0075).
- [ ] An ADR records the procedure.
