# Support rota and incident playbook

Status: ready-for-human
Type: task
Blocked by: none

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least two named people cover the ADR 0030 and ADR 0067 windows: general support 8 AM–8 PM, check-in cover until midnight, and 24/7 on-call during active stays.
- [ ] Written playbooks for: failed access, power or water outage, safety or security incident, unit not as described, guest who will not leave, and no-show.
- [ ] Each playbook names who decides on relocation or refund, and their approval limit (issue 07).
