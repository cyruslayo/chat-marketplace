# Sign and inspect the first 10–20 units

Status: ready-for-human
Type: task
Blocked by: 03

## What to do

There is no real inventory yet: the rehearsal has two units from one pretend operator, with stock photos. Sign real owners, clustered in two or three neighbourhoods per city (for example Lekki Phase 1, VI and Ikoyi; Wuse 2, Maitama and Jabi) so one person can reach any unit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least 10 owners interviewed, with their answers on rate, payout timing, calendar sharing and pain points recorded (replaces issue 42's test data).
- [ ] 10–20 units with signed owner agreements and management authority.
- [ ] Each unit physically inspected against the ADR 0008 checklist, with real photos and a short video taken by us.
- [ ] Each unit's agreed amounts and margin rate entered in the inventory file (guest-payments P4).
