# Engage Nigerian counsel for the launch legal pack

Status: ready-for-human
Type: task
Blocked by: 01

## What to do

Hire a Lagos firm (fixed fee if possible) to confirm the curated B2B2C model in ADR 0089 is lawful and to draft the documents. ADR 0006 and ADR 0089 both require this before paid launch.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Written advice on the platform collecting all guest funds and paying owners later (collection agent, not escrow), including any payments-regulation exposure.
- [ ] Owner agreement: agreed amounts, payout timing (ADR 0089), cancellation outcomes, calendar authority, management authority (ADR 0010), on-site duties.
- [ ] Guest terms naming the accommodation provider, the cancellation catalogue (ADR 0058), house rules (ADR 0059) and the protection promise (issue 07).
- [ ] Privacy notice and Nigeria Data Protection Act registration status.
- [ ] Lagos State and FCT shortlet registration or licensing requirements confirmed for the pilot units.
- [ ] Tax position (VAT on margin, withholding) confirmed.
- [ ] Counsel's name, firm and advice date recorded here.
