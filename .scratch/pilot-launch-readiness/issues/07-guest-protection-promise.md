# Fund and state the guest protection promise

Status: ready-for-human
Type: grilling
Blocked by: 01

## What to decide

ADR 0089 moved the Guest Protection Fund out of launch scope, but "we move you or refund you" is the core customer promise. Decide how much to set aside, the per-incident caps, and who approves payouts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed amount decided and held in a separate account (ADR 0027 and ADR 0063 give provisional figures to start from).
- [ ] Per-incident relocation and refund caps decided.
- [ ] One-line guest-facing promise approved by counsel (issue 03).
- [ ] An ADR records the launch decision, amending ADR 0089's "out of launch scope" line.
