# Evidence matrix (Phase 0)

Filled in by issues 01 to 04 and consolidated by 05 (12 Nov 2026). One row per observation, merged where sources describe the same defect. Keep what you saw (Observed) separate from what it means (Guideline rule, Priority). Source ids: `01#n` = `evidence/01-rows.md` row n, `02#n` = `02-rows.md` (A1 to A18, B01 to B17 and N1 to N3 are the audit IDs in the Observed text), `03#n` = `03-rows.md`, `04#n` = `04-rows.md`.

Conventions:
- **Surface:** the names in guideline §5 (Landing, Discovery, Unit detail, Draft, Review, Request sent, Offer, Payment choice, Payment pending, Reservation confirmed, Expired/declined, Operator inbox, Operator detail), plus Extended Stay schedule. Contact, Error and Tooling are extra.
- **Channel:** chat, standard route, no-JS, or the operator's route.
- **Width:** 320, 390, 550 (the issue 04 pane), 768 or 1280, plus 200% zoom where relevant.
- **State:** normal, empty, busy, invalid, expired, declined, pending, confirmed, failure.
- **Source:** new (found in Phase 0), or for an older audit finding its status: fixed, current, partial or unverified.
- **Priority:** guideline §14 (P0 to P3). `—` means not a defect (a conforming row or a coverage note). Where a priority is raised or lowered from the source row, the reason is written in the Observed cell.
- **Issue:** an issue number, `no action` (not a defect, or owned by the owner decision of 12 Nov 2026), or `proposed: <title>` for a finding no open issue covers. Rows marked `31` are checks that must pass before release sign-off.
