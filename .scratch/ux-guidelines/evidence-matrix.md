# Evidence matrix (Phase 0)

Filled in by issues 01 to 04 and consolidated by 05. One row per observation. Keep what you saw (Observed) separate from what it means (Guideline rule, Priority).

Column values:
- **Surface:** the names in guideline §5 (Landing, Discovery, Unit detail, Draft, Review, Request sent, Offer, Payment choice, Payment pending, Reservation confirmed, Expired/declined, Operator inbox, Operator detail), plus Extended Stay schedule.
- **Channel:** chat, standard route or no-JS.
- **Width:** 320, 390, 768 or 1280, plus 200% zoom where relevant.
- **State:** normal, empty, busy, invalid, expired, stale, failure.
- **Source:** new (found in Phase 0), or for an older audit finding (A1 to A18, browser-audit B01 to B17), its status: fixed, current, partial or unverified.
- **Priority:** guideline §14 (P0 to P3).
