# 29 · Unassisted task study

Status: ready-for-human
Type: research
Blocked by: 05
Phase: 5 (Batch E)
Applicable ADRs: guideline §13

## What to build

Prepare and run the first task study.

- At least two Guest profiles and one Operator profile, five participants in total, on real phones where possible, including a small Android phone on a weak connection, and keyboard and screen-reader users.
- Write `.scratch/ux-guidelines/study/protocol.md`: the nine tasks from §13, a consent script, an observation sheet (completion, wrong turns, hesitation, misunderstanding, recovery, quotes) and synthetic data only.
- Results go to `study/results.md` with evidence kept apart from interpretation, and new findings go into the matrix.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The protocol covers all nine tasks with a consent script
2. Each session's observations are recorded per task
3. New P0 and P1 findings are in the matrix with an issue number

## Rules

- The owner recruits and runs the sessions; agents prepare documents only. Never record personal data beyond consented notes.
