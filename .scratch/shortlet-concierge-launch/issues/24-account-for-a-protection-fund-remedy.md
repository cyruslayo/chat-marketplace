# Fund and account for an approved Guest Protection Fund remedy

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: AFK
User stories: 56–57, 89, 91

## Parent

[Concierge Platform and Verified Shortlet Launch PRD](../PRD.md)

## What to build

Fund an approved guest remedy from the Guest Protection Fund when ordinary recovery is unavailable, record the contribution, exposure, payment, Operator liability, and recovery rights, and prevent the fund from replacing ordinary responsible-party obligations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Seed, contribution, target, approval, and available-balance rules use versioned provisional policy.
- [ ] Every movement posts balanced ledger entries with incident, booking, decision, and funding correlation.
- [ ] Insufficient fund balance does not erase the approved guest remedy or Refund Fallback workflow.
- [ ] Finance can see exposure and recovery without accessing unrelated interaction or identity data.
