# Submit and answer an evidence-backed deposit claim

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: AFK
User stories: 59–60

## Parent

[Concierge Platform and Verified Shortlet Launch PRD](../PRD.md)

## What to build

Open a Security Deposit Claim after authoritative checkout, require timely itemized Operator evidence, achieve Successful Claim Notification, give the guest a 48-hour Claim Response Window, and let an authorized human decide on the Balance of Evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Claim timing begins from amended checkout where applicable and rejects unsupported or late submissions under policy.
- [ ] Notification status is evidence-backed; the response period starts only after successful notice.
- [ ] The Operator bears the full proof burden and cannot rely on arbitrary fees or uncorroborated assertions.
- [ ] Claim, response, evidence provenance, reserved amount, decision, and notices remain auditable and tenant-scoped.
