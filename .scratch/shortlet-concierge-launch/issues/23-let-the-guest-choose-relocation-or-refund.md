# Let the guest choose relocation or Refund Fallback

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: AFK
User stories: 54–57

## Parent

[Concierge Platform and Verified Shortlet Launch PRD](../PRD.md)

## What to build

For a qualifying failure, present the Primary Guest with comparable relocation candidates and a full-refund choice, enforce bounded human spending authority, capture explicit selection, and guarantee Refund Fallback when an acceptable replacement cannot be completed.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Replacement comparison preserves capacity, location, quality, safety, dates, price difference, transport, and material disclosures.
- [ ] Routine, senior, and executive relocation limits require the accepted human roles and approvals.
- [ ] The guest is never forced to relocate and temporary substitution requires consent.
- [ ] Choice, funding source, Operator liability, booking consequences, and resulting projection are committed atomically and audited.
