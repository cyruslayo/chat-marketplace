Status: ready-for-human
Type: HITL
User stories: 34–62, 73–77, 83–93, 103–104

## Parent

[Concierge Platform and Verified Shortlet Launch PRD](../PRD.md)

## What to build

Run staffed operational simulations for request delivery, payment expiry and late success, same-day arrival and turnover, failed access, relocation, Mid-Stay Failure, cancellation, No-Show, deposit claims, overstay, Operator enforcement, provider outage, Human Handoff, and return to automation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every scenario has named participants, clocked targets, injected failures, observed actions, authoritative outcome, and debrief findings.
- [ ] General, check-in, and active-stay emergency coverage meets the accepted ownership and escalation targets.
- [ ] Humans can recover and reconcile each scenario through authorized application routes without direct state manipulation.
- [ ] Material gaps receive owners and blocking severity; launch cannot pass on undocumented workarounds.
