Components

  • Task bar, Stay ticket, Price breakdown
  • Steps, Banner (warning, danger), Outcome, Status word
  • Button, Action bar with two actions

Not a danger button: danger is for declines and failures. The consequence is in words above the button and the safe choice sits beside it.
