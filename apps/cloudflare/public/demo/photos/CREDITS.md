# Demo photos

Illustrative photographs for the staging demo's synthetic apartments (ADR 0105). They show interiors and buildings of the
kind a unit could have. They do not depict any unit, building or operator in the demo. Every unit is labelled "Demo listing".

Each file was downloaded from `images.unsplash.com` on 22 Oct 2026, resized to 1600 pixels wide and saved as JPEG. The
photographer and page were taken from the Unsplash page for that photo. All photos are licensed under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The one centre-crop is noted in its row.
